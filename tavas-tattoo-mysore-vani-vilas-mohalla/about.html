Tavas Tattoo Mysore

Step into Tavas Tattoo Mysore in Vani Vilas Mohalla and feel at home from the first hello. Talk through your idea, then let's make it yours, fine line or bold realism.

Tavas Tattoo Mysore is a tattoo studio in Vani Vilas Mohalla, Mysuru, where every design starts as a conversation. Bring a rough idea or a reference photo, and we'll shape it into something you'll want on your skin for a long time.

Our work runs from delicate fine line pieces to detailed realism and fully custom designs. Whatever you choose, it's made for you, one session at a time, with the care a permanent mark deserves.
