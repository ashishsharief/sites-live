Artivine by Nivi

Walk in, pick a design or bring your own idea, and Nivi takes it from sketch to finished work. Relaxed, unhurried, and precise.

Artivine is run by Niveditha Prasad, known as Nivi, in Gokulam, Mysuru. It is a nail art and tattoo studio where each design is made around the person who will wear it, not picked off a wall.

Come in with a rough idea or a photo you love. Nivi listens first, then shapes it into something that feels like you. Rated 4.3 by visitors.
