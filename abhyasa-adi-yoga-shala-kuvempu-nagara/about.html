Abhyasa Adi Yoga Shala

Step into Abhyasa Adi Yoga Shala in Kuvempu Nagara, Mysuru, and find a calm room to practise at your own pace. Rated 5.0.

Abhyasa means steady practice, and that is the heart of Abhyasa Adi Yoga Shala in Kuvempu Nagara, Mysuru. It is a yoga studio built for people who want to keep coming back to their mat.

New to yoga or returning after a break, you are welcome here. Come as you are, practise with patience, and let one steady session at a time do its work.
