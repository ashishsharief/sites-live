Bakes and Beyond Mysuru

Bakes and Beyond in Subhashnagar is all about handcrafted eggless desserts, from fudgy brownies to sticky toffee pudding. Plan a little ahead: pre-orders open two days in advance.

Bakes and Beyond Mysuru is a bakery with a clear focus: handcrafted, eggless desserts. We don't do celebration cakes, so the counter is all brownies, cookies and puddings.

Our customers have rated us 5.0 on Google, and we are FSSAI registered. Pre-orders need two days' notice, so plan ahead and let us bake for you.
