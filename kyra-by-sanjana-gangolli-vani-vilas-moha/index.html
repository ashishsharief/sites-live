About

Tell Kyra what you have in mind and leave feeling like yourself. Kyra by Sanjana Gangolli is a makeup artist in Vani Vilas Mohalla, for brides, event guests and expecting mothers.

Kyra's work covers the whole wedding day. Airbrush bridal makeup and South Indian bridal looks sit alongside reception and event makeup, so the look can carry from the ceremony to the reception. Maternity makeup and nail art are on the list too.

Kyra also teaches makeup classes for anyone keen to learn the craft. Based in Vani Vilas Mohalla and rated 5.0, Kyra is ready for your next big moment, big or small.
