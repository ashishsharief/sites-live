About

Bring a wedding, a party, or just a day you want to feel like yourself. Kyra works with you on the look, from airbrush bridal to event makeup.

Kyra by Sanjana Gangolli is a makeup artist in Vani Vilas Mohalla. Brides come for airbrush and South Indian bridal looks, guests for reception and event makeup, and mothers-to-be for maternity makeup.

Makeup classes are also on offer, so you can learn to do the look yourself. Nail art and manicures round out the menu.
