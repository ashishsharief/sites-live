About

Walk into Guna Bakehouse in Gokulam and the smell of fresh bakes greets you first. Pick a cake or a brownie tub to take home, and settle in.

Guna Bakehouse is a bakery in Gokulam, rated 5.0 by its customers. Whether you want fresh bakes for home or a custom cake for a celebration, we bake with care.

Take home Loaded Nutella brownie tubs, Ferrero brownie tubs, or the Matilda chocolate cake for the chocolate lovers in your life. Need something made just for you? Ask for a custom cake and we will make it.
