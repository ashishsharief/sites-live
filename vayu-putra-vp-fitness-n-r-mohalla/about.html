Vayu Putra (VP) Fitness

Step into Vayu Putra Fitness in N R Mohalla, Rajendra Nagar, a unisex gym built around purposeful training. Cardio, weights and a focused floor, with room to grow at your own pace.

At VP Fitness, training is built around you. Whether you're here for cardio to get your heart pumping, bodybuilding to build muscle, or a personal trainer to guide every rep, the floor is open to everyone.

Goals change, and VP Fitness keeps up. Weight gain and weight loss diet plans, plus protein supplements, sit alongside your training so nutrition and workouts move together. Start where you are.
