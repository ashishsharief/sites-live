About

Fika Patisserie Mysuru

Walk into Fika Patisserie Mysuru in Siddartha Nagar and pick a cookie tin for the family, or order a custom cake for the day that matters.

Fika makes eggless cakes so more people at your table can share a slice. Cookie tins are easy to give, and custom cakes are made around the occasion you have in mind.

Planning ahead? Pre-order your desserts and we will have them ready for you. Tell us what you are celebrating and we will shape a cake around it.
