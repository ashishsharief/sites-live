Om Photoverse

Walk into Om Photoverse on IPS Nagar, Kadakola, and it feels like a studio built for your story. Bring your people, your plans, your everyday moments; we take it from there.

Om Photoverse is a photography studio in IPS Nagar, Kadakola, Mysuru. We shoot the moments that matter most: weddings and engagements, pre-wedding and maternity sessions, and the everyday work that helps businesses show up, from car deliveries to product and real estate shoots.

Whether it is your wedding day, a new baby on the way, or a car ready for its new owner, we aim to make each frame feel like you. Share your idea, and we will help bring it to life.
