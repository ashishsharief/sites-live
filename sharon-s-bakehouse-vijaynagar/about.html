Sharon's Bakehouse

Step into Sharon's Bakehouse in Vijaynagar, Mysuru, and the warmth of the bakes greets you first. Pick something for now, something for later, and settle in.

Sharon's Bakehouse is a neighbourhood bakery in Vijaynagar, Mysuru, made for everyday moments: a quick stop on the way home, a treat with your evening tea. Every visit should feel personal, like walking into a kitchen that knows your name.

Come by for your usual or something new to try. Stop to chat, take your time, and let us help you choose. We hope you leave a little happier than you arrived and already planning your next visit.
