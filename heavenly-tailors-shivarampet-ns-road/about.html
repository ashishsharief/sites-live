About

Heavenly Tailors

Walk into Heavenly Tailors on NS Road, Shivarampet, and you'll feel right at home. Family run since 1964, we take every measurement with care so each piece fits you.

Heavenly Tailors has been cutting bespoke menswear since 1964. It is still family run, and the same care that began in those early years shapes every suit, blazer and sherwani that leaves the shop today.

The shop tailors to the Mysore royal family, and it's rated 4.7 on Google. Come in for a bandhgala, a wedding sherwani or a sharp pair of trousers, and leave feeling like yourself, only better dressed.
